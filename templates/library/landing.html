Question:
What is the difference between elastic and inelastic collisions?
Grounded Explanation:

According to your lecture notes, total linear momentum is conserved in both types of collisions. However, in an elastic collision, kinetic energy is strictly conserved. In an inelastic collision, part of the initial kinetic energy is converted into thermal energy, deformation, or sound.
