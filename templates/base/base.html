StudyVault Assistant Guide

1. Grounded In Your Documents

StudyVault uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Every answer is synthesized exclusively from your uploaded study materials, with page citations and exact text excerpts.

2. Strict Factual Honesty

If your uploaded notes don't contain sufficient context to answer a question, the assistant honestly abstains rather than hallucinating facts.

3. Scope Your Inquiries

Target your entire library, a specific subject collection, or individual documents using the scope selector at the bottom or top of the chat.

4. Keyboard Shortcuts

Enter Send question • Shift+Enter New line • Ctrl+K Search saved chats
